      How to do it

      1. Pick the document. In the photo tool, tap the document you need (for example Photo or Signature). The JEE Main 2027 limits are filled in.
      2. Choose your image. Choose the photo or scanned signature from your phone or computer (JPG, PNG or WebP).
      3. Check the result. The tool saves a JPG inside the KB range, at the pixel size or shape where the notice gives one. Check that the preview is clear.
      4. Download and upload. Download the JPG and upload it in the JEE Main 2027 application form.

      Common reasons uploads are rejected

      • Over the maximum KB: the portal refuses the file. The tool keeps it under the limit.
      • Under the minimum KB: some portals refuse very small files. The tool adds invisible padding if needed, without changing the picture.
      • Wrong format: photos and signatures must be JPG/JPEG as the notice says; certificates must be PDF where the table says PDF. Phone photos in HEIC or PNG need converting, which the tool does.
      • Blurry or dark scan: retake it in good light before resizing. Resizing cannot add detail that is not there.
      • Photo: a recent passport-size colour photo with about 80% face visible, including ears, without a mask, against a white background.
      • The form also captures a live photo with your webcam or phone: use a light-coloured background, good light, ears visible, no mask.
      • Every file must be clearly legible: the bulletin says so for each upload.

      Exam-specific points are from the NTA Information Bulletin, JEE (Main) 2027.

      Is my photo or PDF uploaded to a server?

      No. Both tools on this page work inside your browser on your own device. The page has no analytics and sets no cookies.

      Is 1 KB counted as 1000 or 1024 bytes?

      Portals differ, so the photo tool keeps the file under the maximum counted as 1000 bytes per KB and over the minimum counted as 1024 bytes per KB. It passes either way.
