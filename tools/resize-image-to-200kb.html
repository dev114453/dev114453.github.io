Questions

How do I reduce a photo to 200 KB?

Choose the photo above. The tool automatically searches for the highest JPG quality that stays under 200 KB, then lets you download it.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. The photo is resized and compressed by your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server, so it works for ID photos and documents.

Why does the photo look smaller in pixels?

If a size limit cannot be reached by compression alone, the tool also reduces the pixel dimensions a little, keeping the shape of the photo.

Which formats work?

JPG, PNG and WebP. The result is saved as JPG, which is what most upload forms ask for.
