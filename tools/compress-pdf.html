Questions

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The PDF is compressed by your own browser, so private documents stay on your device.

How does it make the PDF smaller?

Each page is redrawn as an optimised image. This works best for scanned documents and photo-heavy PDFs. Text becomes part of the image, so it can no longer be selected.

Which setting should I use?

"Balanced" suits most documents. Use "Smallest file" for strict upload limits and "Best quality" when small print must stay sharp.
