China Visa Photo Online (420 × 560 px, 40–120 KB)

The Chinese visa online form asks for a digital photo of 354–420 px wide by 472–560 px tall, 40 to 120 KB. This tool crops to that shape and saves a 420 × 560 px JPEG under 120 KB.

Choose a photo or drop it here

Your photo never leaves this device.

Official requirements

  • 354–420 × 472–560 px, JPEG, 40–120 KB.
  • White background, face straight on, ears visible, no glasses.
  • The printed size is 33 × 48 mm.

Source: Chinese visa application photo requirements. Rules can change; always check the official page before you submit.

Questions

Does this check that my photo will be accepted?

No. It sets the correct size, shape and file size. You still need to follow the official rules on background, pose and lighting.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Cropping and compression happen in your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

The face is cut off. What do I do?

Use a photo with space around the head, or switch "Position" to "Centre". The tool keeps the top of the photo by default, where the head usually is.
