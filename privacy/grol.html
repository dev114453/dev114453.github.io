Privacy Policy — GROL Exam Prep – FCC 1, 3 & 8

Last updated: 19 November 2026

GROL Exam Prep – FCC 1, 3 & 8 ("the app") (package name com.developer1021.grol) is an Android application published on Google Play by the developer developer1021 for studying for the US FCC commercial radio operator license exams (Elements 1, 3 and 8). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data collected

The app does not require an account. It does not use crash reporting; the usage analytics it does use are described below. Your answer history and practice exam scores are stored only in the app's private storage on your device and are removed if you use "Reset progress" or uninstall the app. The developer does not collect, receive or store your name, email or other personal details.

Usage analytics

From version 1.6 the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count a small set of usage events: when the upgrade screen is shown and from which screen, when a purchase is started, completed or fails (product, price and currency, never payment details), and first-use milestones such as finishing your first practice exam (with a score band such as "80-89", never your answers). These events are linked only to a random app-instance ID that Firebase creates on your device and that is reset when you reinstall the app. The app turns off collection of the advertising ID and of ad personalisation signals for analytics, and it sends no name, email, contacts, files, recordings or other personal content. Google processes this data on the developer's behalf as a service provider (Firebase privacy information); the developer uses it only to understand how the app is used and to improve it, and never sells it.

Ads

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (derived from your IP address), app interactions and diagnostic data. Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from "Privacy choices" in the app. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Android settings. Upgrading to Pro removes all ads; once the app knows you have Pro it makes no ad requests.

Purchases

Any optional in-app purchase is processed entirely by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy; we never see or receive your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that a purchase is valid so the upgrade can be unlocked.

Ratings and other apps

After you pass a practice exam, the app may show Google Play's standard rating prompt; any rating or review you give is handled by Google Play. The app may also suggest a related exam app from the same developer. To hide that suggestion when the app is already installed, it checks on your device only; nothing is sent to us.

Permissions

The app only accesses files or media you explicitly choose. It uses internet access to load ads and to communicate with Google Play for purchases and ratings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
