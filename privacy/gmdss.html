Privacy Policy — GMDSS Exam Prep

Last updated: 19 November 2026

GMDSS Exam Prep ("the app") is a study app for the US FCC commercial radio operator examinations (Elements 1, 7, 7R and 9), published by the developer developer1021 on Google Play (package name com.developer1021.gmdss). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts or photos, and we do not collect your location ourselves (see Ads below). Your study progress (answers, review schedule, streak, settings, bookmarks and practice-exam history) is stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers and we never receive this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Usage analytics

From version 1.4 the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count a small set of usage events: when the upgrade screen is shown and from which screen, when a purchase is started, completed or fails (product, price and currency, never payment details), and first-use milestones such as finishing your first practice exam (with a score band such as "80-89", never your answers). These events are linked only to a random app-instance ID that Firebase creates on your device and that is reset when you reinstall the app. The app turns off collection of the advertising ID and of ad personalisation signals for analytics, and it sends no name, email, contacts, files, recordings or other personal content. Google processes this data on the developer's behalf as a service provider (Firebase privacy information); the developer uses it only to understand how the app is used and to improve it, and never sells it.

Ads

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (derived from your IP address), app interactions and diagnostic data. Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from "Privacy choices" in the app. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Android settings. Upgrading to Pro removes all ads; once the app knows you have Pro it makes no ad requests.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Pro" upgrade is processed by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details; the app only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase is valid so Pro can be unlocked.

Ratings and other apps

After a passing practice session, the app may show Google Play's standard rating prompt; any rating or review you give is handled by Google Play. The app may also suggest a related exam app from the same developer, and checks on your device only whether that app is already installed. It uses internet access to load ads and to communicate with Google Play for purchases and ratings.

Children

The app is intended for adults and teenagers studying for professional radio licences and is not directed to children under 13.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell or share your data. Data used for ads is processed by Google AdMob as described under Ads; usage events are processed by Google as described under Usage analytics.

Your choices

  • Delete all study data with Settings → Reset all progress, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Change your ad consent choice from "Privacy choices" in the app (shown where required), reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in Android settings, or upgrade to Pro to remove ads.

Independence

Questions come from the public FCC commercial radio operator question pools. The app is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Federal Communications Commission or the US Coast Guard.
