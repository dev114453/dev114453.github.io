Privacy Policy — GMDSS Exam Prep

Last updated: 9 November 2026

GMDSS Exam Prep ("the app") is a study app for the US FCC commercial radio operator examinations (Elements 1, 7, 7R and 9), published by the developer developer1021 on Google Play (package name com.developer1021.gmdss). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location. Your study progress (answers, review schedule, streak, settings, bookmarks and practice-exam history) is stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers and we never receive this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

No ads, no analytics

The app shows no ads and contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting code. It does not use the advertising ID.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Pro" upgrade is processed by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details; the app only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase is valid so Pro can be unlocked.

Ratings and other apps

After a passing practice session, the app may show Google Play's standard rating prompt; any rating or review you give is handled by Google Play. The app may also suggest a related exam app from the same developer, and checks on your device only whether that app is already installed. The app uses internet access only to communicate with Google Play for purchases and ratings.

Children

The app is intended for adults and teenagers studying for professional radio licences and is not directed to children under 13.

Data sharing and security

We do not collect, sell or share your data.

Your choices

  • Delete all study data with Settings → Reset all progress, or by uninstalling the app.

Independence

Questions come from the public FCC commercial radio operator question pools. The app is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Federal Communications Commission or the US Coast Guard.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
