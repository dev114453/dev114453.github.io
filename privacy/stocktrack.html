Stock Alert – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026 (Android version 3.3; iPhone version 1.3)

This policy covers Stock Alert (Google Play listing "Stock Alert: Alarm & Net Worth", package name com.pdfcompress.stocktrack), an Android app published by the developer developer1021. Contact: devloper1021@gmail.com.

Stock Alert ("the app") lets you follow stock prices, get price alerts, keep a portfolio and see your net worth. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information stored on your device

Your watchlist, portfolio entries, price alerts, net-worth items (deposits, retirement accounts, gold, property, cash, loans and other assets you add), family profiles, goals, net-worth history and settings are stored only on your device. Family members are labels you create on your phone; nothing is shared with them or anyone else. We do not run user accounts and we do not upload this data to our own servers. Uninstalling the app deletes it. If Android's own device backup is turned on, Android may include the app's data file in your Google backup, as for other apps.

Importing screenshots, PDFs and spreadsheets

Screenshots and PDFs are read on your phone. They are never uploaded. When you import a screenshot, a PDF statement (including password-protected statements such as CAMS/KFintech CAS) or a CSV/Excel file, the app reads it on your device: text in images and scanned pages is recognised with Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition, whose model is built into the app; PDF text is read with an on-device PDF library. The files, the text read from them and any PDF password you type stay on your phone; the app does not copy the files or store the password, and it only keeps the rows you choose to import. ML Kit is configured in the app so that it cannot send its usage statistics.

Two optional, separate network requests can follow an import, and neither contains your files or the text read from them: if you tap "Find" to match a company name to a stock symbol, the words in the search box are sent to the market data provider, exactly like the app's search box; and when you have Indian mutual funds in Net worth, the app downloads the public AMFI daily NAV file (the same file for everyone, no information about you in the request). After import, prices for the imported symbols are requested like for any stock you track.

App lock and protected backups

App lock uses Android's fingerprint, face or screen-lock check. The app only learns whether the check succeeded; it never receives biometric data. Backups you export can be protected with a password: the file is encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM, key derived from your password with PBKDF2). The password is never stored or sent. If you forget it, the backup cannot be opened.

Market data requests

To show prices, charts and news, the app requests market data for the stock symbols you view or track (including those in your portfolio and net worth, such as gold and crypto prices and currency rates) from a third-party market data provider (currently Yahoo Finance). These requests contain the stock symbol and standard technical information (such as your IP address) that any internet request includes. They do not contain your name, contacts, quantities, amounts or net worth. Indian mutual fund prices come from AMFI's public daily file, as described above.

Usage analytics

From version 3.3 the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count a small set of usage events: when the upgrade screen is shown and from which screen, when a purchase is started, completed or fails (product, price and currency, never payment details), and first-use milestones such as creating your first alert and your first alert going off. These events are linked only to a random app-instance ID that Firebase creates on your device and that is reset when you reinstall the app. The app turns off collection of the advertising ID and of ad personalisation signals for analytics, and it sends no name, email, contacts, files, recordings or other personal content. Google processes this data on the developer's behalf as a service provider (Firebase privacy information); the developer uses it only to understand how the app is used and to improve it, and never sells it.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data to serve, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from the app's settings ("Privacy choices"). See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Stock Alert Pro subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether you have an active subscription.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows price-alert notifications generated on your device. Alert checks run on your device in the background. On iPhone, alerts can also be checked by our push server when Instant alerts is on (see "Instant alerts on iPhone" below). The optional morning summary and the monthly "update your values" reminder are also created on your device; with app lock and "Hide amounts" on, the morning summary shows percentages only.

Instant alerts on iPhone (server push)

The iPhone version of Stock Alert (bundle ID com.pdfcompress.stocktrack) has an optional feature called Instant alerts, which is on by default when you allow notifications. Because iPhone only lets apps check in the background now and then, a small server run by the developer checks your alert rules every minute while the relevant market is open and sends the notification through Apple Push Notification service (APNs), so alerts arrive even when the app is closed.

For this, the app sends to the server: a random device ID and a random secret created by the app (used only to sign the app's requests), the push token Apple gives the app, your time zone and language, the app version, whether you have Stock Alert Pro, and your alert rules: the stock symbols, the alert type and thresholds, on/off and snooze state, the symbols of a watchlist used by a watchlist alert, the alarm tone, and the notification text in your language. It does not send your name, email, contacts, portfolio, quantities, amounts or net worth, and there is no account. The server looks up prices for the symbols in your rules from the market data provider, without any information about you.

The server keeps your rules for as long as Instant alerts is on, and a log of alerts it sent for 14 days so the app can show them in History. Turning off Instant alerts in the app's Settings deletes your device ID, push token and rules from the server; devices that have not connected for 180 days are deleted automatically. This data is not linked to your identity, not used for tracking or advertising, and never sold or shared, except with the service providers needed to run the feature: Cloudflare (hosting) and Apple (delivering the notification).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Not investment advice

Prices may be delayed or inaccurate. The app is for information only and does not provide investment advice or trading services.
