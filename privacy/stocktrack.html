Stock Alert – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Stock Alert ("the app") lets you follow stock prices, keep a watchlist and portfolio, and get price alerts. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information stored on your device

Your watchlist, portfolio entries, price alerts and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run user accounts and we do not upload this data to our own servers. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Market data requests

To show prices, charts and news, the app requests market data for the stock symbols you view or track from a third-party market data provider (currently Yahoo Finance). These requests contain the stock symbol and standard technical information (such as your IP address) that any internet request includes. They do not contain your name, contacts or portfolio quantities.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data to serve, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from the app's settings ("Privacy choices"). See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Stock Alert Pro subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether you have an active subscription.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows price-alert notifications generated on your device. Alert checks run on your device in the background.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Not investment advice

Prices may be delayed or inaccurate. The app is for information only and does not provide investment advice or trading services.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
