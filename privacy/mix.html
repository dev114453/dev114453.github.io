Privacy Policy — Mix: Color Match Puzzle

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Mix: Color Match Puzzle ("the app", com.developer1021.mixcolor) is a puzzle game published by the developer developer1021 on Google Play and the Apple App Store. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data collected

The app does not require an account. Your game progress is stored only on your device.

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID on Android or IDFA on iPhone, only if you allow tracking), IP address, coarse location and ad interaction data to show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice in the app.

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play or Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
