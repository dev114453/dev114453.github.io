Privacy Policy — Inner Sky: Meditation & Sleep

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Inner Sky ("the app", formerly called Vast) is a free meditation, breathing, sleep-sound and music app for iPhone and Android, published by the developer developer1021. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

The short version: the app does not collect any personal data. Nothing you do in the app leaves your device.

What stays on your device

Your practice history (dates and lengths of sessions), lesson and course progress, the optional free-days counter in the Freedom section, your sound mix and your settings are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded by the app. Deleting the app removes them. If your device's own backup (iCloud or Google backup) is turned on, the operating system may include this local data in your personal device backup, under your account and the platform's privacy terms.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

There is no sign-up or login. The app shows no advertising and contains no advertising SDKs. It contains no third-party analytics and does not track you across apps or websites.

No purchases

Inner Sky is completely free. It has no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.

Microphone

The humming-breath (Bhramari) lesson can listen to your hum to give live feedback, only after you tap "Practise with feedback" and allow microphone access. The sound is analysed on your device while the lesson is open; it is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled locally on your device. No notification server is involved.

Spoken guidance

Narration is either built into the app or generated on your device with the system's text-to-speech voices. No audio or text is sent anywhere.

Health information

The app is a wellbeing tool, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice. It does not read from or write to Apple Health or Health Connect.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
