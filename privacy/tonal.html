GlowShop Photo Editor – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

GlowShop (formerly called Tonal) is a photo editor for Android published by developer1021. This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: GlowShop does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. Your photos never leave your phone. If you buy GlowShop Pro, the purchase is processed by Google Play, not by us.

Your photos

Photos you open in GlowShop are processed entirely on your device. GlowShop only gets access to the photos you pick through Android's photo picker. Your photos are never uploaded anywhere.

Edits saved on your device

So you can come back to an edit, GlowShop keeps your edit settings and a small preview image in the app's private storage on your phone. Edited photos you export are saved as new images in your gallery (Pictures/GlowShop; older exports stay in Pictures/Tonal). Your original photos are never changed. You can remove saved edits from the app's home screen, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

On-device features

"AI Select Person", "AI Select Background" and background blur use a machine-learning model (Google ML Kit selfie segmentation) that is bundled inside the app and runs on your device. No image is sent anywhere to use them.

GlowShop Pro purchases (Google Play Billing)

GlowShop is free to use. Optional GlowShop Pro (a yearly subscription with a free trial, or a one-time lifetime purchase) is sold through Google Play Billing. When you buy, subscribe, start a trial or tap "Restore purchase", the app talks to the Google Play Store app on your phone, and Google processes the payment under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

We never see or receive your name, email address, card or other payment details. The app only receives a confirmation from Google Play that Pro is owned (which product, whether it is active, and a purchase token used to acknowledge it), and it keeps that confirmation on your phone so Pro also works offline. We do not run a server that stores purchases. Google Play gives developers aggregate sales reports and order records for refunds and tax as part of operating Google Play; we use them only for that.

You can cancel a subscription at any time in the Google Play Store (Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions). If you cancel during the free trial, you are not charged.

Network use

GlowShop itself does not connect to the internet: photo editing works fully offline. Purchases and purchase checks are handled by the Google Play Store app, which uses its own connection.

No accounts, ads or tracking

GlowShop has no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

Permissions

On Android 9 and older, GlowShop asks for storage permission only to save exported photos to your gallery. On newer versions no runtime permission is needed. The app also declares Google Play's billing permission (com.android.vending.BILLING), which lets it offer GlowShop Pro through Google Play.

Children

GlowShop is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
