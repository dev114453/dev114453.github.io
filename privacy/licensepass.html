Privacy Policy — LicensePass

Last updated: 18 November 2026

LicensePass ("the app", bundle ID com.developer1021.licensepass) is an iPhone app by the developer developer1021 for studying for the US amateur radio (Technician, General, Extra) and FCC commercial (GROL, RADAR, GMDSS) license exams.

Data collected

The app does not require an account and does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking. Your answers, practice exam scores, bookmarks and settings are stored only on your iPhone and are removed if you use "Reset all progress" or delete the app. The developer does not collect, receive or store any personal data.

Purchases

If the app offers an optional in-app purchase, it is processed by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policy; we never see your payment details.

Ratings and feedback

The app may show Apple's standard rating prompt. "Send feedback" opens your own email app addressed to us; nothing is sent unless you send that email.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
