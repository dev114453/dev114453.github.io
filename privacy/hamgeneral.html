Privacy Policy — Ham Radio General Class Exam

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Ham Radio General Class Exam ("the app") (package name com.developer1021.hamgeneral) is an Android application published on Google Play by the developer developer1021 for studying for the US amateur radio General class license exam. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

iOS version. The app is also available for iPhone on the Apple App Store under the same name. The iOS version handles your information in the same way described here, except that purchases are processed by Apple instead of Google Play.

Data collected

The app does not require an account and does not use analytics. Your answer history and practice exam scores are stored only in the app's private storage on your device and are removed if you use "Reset progress" or uninstall the app.

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID on Android or IDFA on iPhone, only if you allow tracking), IP address, coarse location and ad interaction data to show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice in the app. Ads are not shown after you buy the optional upgrade.

Purchases

Any optional in-app purchase is processed entirely by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy; we never see your payment details.

Permissions

The app only accesses files or media you explicitly choose. It uses internet access for purchases and to load ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
