Privacy Policy — PDF Crush: Compress PDF Size

Last updated: 17 October 2026

PDF Crush: Compress PDF Size ("the app") (package name com.pdfcompress.start) is an Android application published on Google Play by the developer developer1021 for compressing PDF files on your phone. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

iOS version. The app is also available for iPhone on the Apple App Store under the same name. The iOS version handles your information in the same way described here, except that purchases are processed by Apple instead of Google Play. The iOS version contains no ads.

Data collected

The app does not require an account. PDF files you choose are processed on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from partner apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Any optional in-app purchase is processed entirely by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy; we never see your payment details.

Permissions

The app only accesses files or media you explicitly choose. It uses internet access to load ads and communicate with Google Play.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
