Privacy Policy

FitMyPDF (formerly PDF Crush) for Android. Last updated: November 15, 2026 (version 3.4)

Short version: your PDFs and photos are processed on your phone and are never uploaded, shared or seen by us. The app is free with ads, shown by Google AdMob. A one-time "Remove ads" purchase turns ads off for good.

1. Who we are

FitMyPDF is an Android app made by an independent developer. This policy explains what data the app and the services it uses handle.

2. Your files

Compressing, merging, splitting, scanning, converting and reading all happen on your device. The app never uploads, transmits or stores your files or their content anywhere. Results are saved where you choose (by default Downloads/FitMyPDF). We have no account system and do not collect your name, email or phone number.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep FitMyPDF free, it shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen and, occasionally, a full-screen ad after you have saved or shared a finished file. AdMob is provided by Google and may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in Android settings),
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • information about the ads shown and your interaction with them,
  • app and device information and diagnostics (crash and performance data of the ad SDK).

This data is used for showing, measuring and improving ads, and for fraud prevention. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and where else the law requires it, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy choices.

4. Remove ads (Google Play)

The optional one-time "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Google Play on your device whether the purchase exists, to switch ads off and to restore it on a new phone.

5. Document scanner (Google Play services)

The optional "Scan to PDF" feature uses the ML Kit document scanner in Google Play services. Scanning runs on your device and the pages are handed only to FitMyPDF. Google Play services may download the scanner module and process limited diagnostic information under the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: only for ads and Google Play (never for your files).
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob as described above.
  • Notifications and foreground service: progress for long jobs, with a Cancel button.
  • Files and photos: only the files you pick through the Android pickers.

7. Data on your device

A short list of recent files (name, sizes, date) and your settings stay on your device. You can clear the list in the app; uninstalling removes it.

8. Children

FitMyPDF is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes

Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new date.
