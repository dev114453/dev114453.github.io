Privacy Policy — FitMyPDF: Compress PDF to KB

Last updated: 11 November 2026 (FitMyPDF 3.3)

FitMyPDF was formerly PDF Crush. The app was renamed in version 3.3; it is the same app with the same package name, and nothing about how it handles your data changed.

Short version: FitMyPDF works entirely on your device. It has no internet permission, shows no ads, has no account, and never uploads, shares or sees your files.

FitMyPDF: Compress PDF to KB ("the app", formerly PDF Crush) (package name com.pdfcompress.start) is an Android application published on Google Play by the developer developer1021 for compressing and converting PDF files on your phone. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data we do not collect

FitMyPDF does not:

  • upload, transmit or store your PDF files or images anywhere;
  • collect your name, email, phone number or any other personal details;
  • require an account or sign-in;
  • track your location;
  • use analytics, crash-reporting or advertising code, or the advertising ID.

The app does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot send data over the network itself.

No ads

Version 3.0 and later show no ads and contain no advertising code. There are no in-app purchases.

Document scanner (Google Play services)

The optional "Scan to PDF" feature uses the ML Kit document scanner provided by Google Play services. Scanning runs on your device and the scanned pages are handed only to FitMyPDF. Google Play services may download the scanner module and may process limited diagnostic information under the Google Privacy Policy. If you do not use the scanner, it is never started.

Permissions and files

  • Notifications: to show progress for long jobs, with a Cancel button.
  • Background work: a foreground service keeps large files compressing while the screen is off.
  • Files and photos: only the files you pick or share to the app, through the Android file and photo pickers. Results are saved on your device only (PDFs in Downloads/FitMyPDF, images in Pictures/FitMyPDF; versions before 3.3 used Downloads/PDF Crush and Pictures/PDF Crush). Your original file is never changed.
  • Camera: FitMyPDF has no camera permission. Tapping Camera opens your camera app, which hands the photo you take to FitMyPDF.

Ratings and other apps

The app may show Google Play's standard rating prompt; any rating or review you give is handled by Google Play. It may also suggest other apps from the same developer, and checks on your device only whether they are already installed.

Local history and settings

FitMyPDF keeps a short list of recent jobs (file name, sizes, date) and your settings on your device so you can find results again. This never leaves your device. You can clear the history in the app, and uninstalling the app removes it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
