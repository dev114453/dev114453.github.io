Privacy Policy — Bhakti Path (संपूर्ण हिंदू भक्ति पाठ)

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Bhakti Path ("the app") (package name com.developer1021.bhaktipath) is an Android application published on Google Play by the developer developer1021 for reading Hindu devotional texts: chalisa, aarti, mantra, stotram, Sunderkand, Bhagavad Gita, vrat katha and Garud Puran. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data collected

The app does not require an account and does not use analytics. All texts are bundled inside the app and work offline.

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID on Android or IDFA on iPhone, only if you allow tracking), IP address, coarse location and ad interaction data to show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice in the app. Ads are not shown after you buy the optional upgrade.

Data stored on your device

Your bookmarks, reading position, font size, reader colour and reminder time are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Permissions

  • Internet — to load ads and process the optional purchase. The texts themselves work offline.
  • Notifications — only to show the daily reading reminder you turn on in Settings.
  • Run at startup / exact alarms — only to restore your reminder after the phone restarts and deliver it at the time you chose.

Children

The app is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Ads shown are subject to Google's policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
