Privacy Policy — Bhakti Path (संपूर्ण हिंदू भक्ति पाठ)

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Bhakti Path ("the app") is a free app by the developer developer1021 for reading and listening to Hindu devotional texts: chalisa, aarti, mantra, stotram, Sunderkand, Bhagavad Gita, vrat katha and more. This policy covers the Android app (package name com.developer1021.bhaktipath, version 4.0 and later) and the iPhone app.

Summary

The app collects no data. It has no ads, no analytics, no account and no purchases. The Android app does not have the internet permission, so it cannot send anything from your phone.

Android app

Data collected

None. We do not collect, store on any server, share or sell any information about you. All texts, audio and pictures are inside the app and work offline.

Data stored on your device

Your bookmarks, reading position, jaap counts, font and colour choices, alarm and reminder settings and other preferences are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. If your phone has Android's own device backup turned on, Android may include app settings in that backup; this is controlled by your Google account settings, not by us.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Notifications: to show the puja-time alarm, the vrat and festival reminders you turn on, and the playback controls while audio is playing.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): to ring your puja alarm and deliver reminders at the time you chose.
  • Run at startup: to set your alarms and reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Foreground service (media playback) and keep awake: to keep an aarti, chalisa or other audio playing when the screen is off or you switch to another app.
  • Vibrate: for the jaap counter and the alarm.
  • Set wallpaper: only when you choose a devotional picture and tap the button to set it as your wallpaper.

The app does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone or storage access, and it does not use the advertising ID.

Sharing and links

If you tap Share, the text or picture you chose is handed to the app you pick (for example a messaging app). If you tap "Rate the app" or the privacy policy link, Google Play or your browser opens. Those apps follow their own privacy policies.

iPhone app

The iPhone version works the same way: no ads, no analytics, no account, no purchases and no data collected. Bookmarks, reading position, jaap counts and settings stay on your device (and in your own device backup, if you use one). It asks for notification permission only for the alarms and reminders you turn on, and plays audio in the background while you listen.

Children

The app is suitable for a general audience. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Earlier versions

Versions before 4.0 on Android showed ads from Google AdMob and offered a "Remove ads" purchase. Those versions are no longer distributed. Updating to the current version removes ads and the internet permission.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
