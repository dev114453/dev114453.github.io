Privacy Policy — Bhakti Path (संपूर्ण हिंदू भक्ति पाठ)

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Bhakti Path ("the app") is a free app by the developer developer1021 for reading and listening to Hindu devotional texts: chalisa, aarti, mantra, stotram, Sunderkand, Bhagavad Gita, vrat katha and more. This policy covers the Android app (package name com.developer1021.bhaktipath, version 4.0 and later) and the iPhone app.

Summary

The developer collects no personal data and has no server, account or analytics. The free Android app shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect some data as described below. A one-time "Remove ads" purchase turns ads off.

Android app

Data collected by the developer

None. We do not collect, store on any server, share or sell any information about you. All texts, audio and pictures are inside the app and work offline.

Ads

The free app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (derived from your IP address), app interactions and diagnostic data. Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from "Privacy choices" in the app. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Android settings.

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy; we never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase is valid. After that, the app makes no ad requests.

Data stored on your device

Your bookmarks, reading position, jaap counts, font and colour choices, alarm and reminder settings and other preferences are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. If your phone has Android's own device backup turned on, Android may include app settings in that backup; this is controlled by your Google account settings, not by us.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Notifications: to show the puja-time alarm, the vrat and festival reminders you turn on, and the playback controls while audio is playing.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): to ring your puja alarm and deliver reminders at the time you chose.
  • Run at startup: to set your alarms and reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Foreground service (media playback) and keep awake: to keep an aarti, chalisa or other audio playing when the screen is off or you switch to another app.
  • Vibrate: for the jaap counter and the alarm.
  • Set wallpaper: only when you choose a devotional picture and tap the button to set it as your wallpaper.

Internet and network state are used only to load ads and to talk to Google Play for the purchase. Advertising ID is used by Google AdMob as described above. The app does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone or storage access.

Sharing and links

If you tap Share, the text or picture you chose is handed to the app you pick (for example a messaging app). If you tap "Rate the app" or the privacy policy link, Google Play or your browser opens. Those apps follow their own privacy policies.

iPhone app

The iPhone version has no ads, no analytics, no account, no purchases and no data collected. Bookmarks, reading position, jaap counts and settings stay on your device (and in your own device backup, if you use one). It asks for notification permission only for the alarms and reminders you turn on, and plays audio in the background while you listen.

Children

The app is suitable for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and ads in the app are limited to content suitable for a general audience. The developer collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Earlier versions

Android versions 4.0 to 4.1 had no ads and no internet permission. Version 4.2 and later show ads in the free version, as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
