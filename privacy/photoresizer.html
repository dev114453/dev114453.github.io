Privacy Policy — Photo & Signature Resizer KB

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Photo & Signature Resizer KB ("the app") (package name com.developer1021.photoresizer) is an Android application published on Google Play by the developer developer1021 for resizing photos and signatures for online forms. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

iOS version. The app is also available for iPhone on the Apple App Store under the same name. The iOS version handles your information in the same way described here, except that purchases are processed by Apple instead of Google Play.

Data collected

None. The app does not require an account and does not use analytics or advertising SDKs. Photos you choose are processed on your device; the resized files are saved only where you choose.

Purchases

Any optional in-app purchase is processed entirely by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy; we never see your payment details.

Permissions

The app only accesses files or media you explicitly choose. It uses internet access to communicate with Google Play for purchases.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
