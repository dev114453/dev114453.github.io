Bhakti Path: Hanuman Chalisa

Hanuman Chalisa, aarti, the Bhagavad Gita and mantra jaap for daily puja. Free, no ads, no login, offline.

About

Bhakti Path is a free Hindi devotional app for daily puja and path. It holds the Hanuman Chalisa and other chalisas, an aarti sangrah, the complete Bhagavad Gita (all 700 shlokas with simple Hindi meaning), Sunderkand, stotras, mantras and vrat kathas. Its Path Mode gently lights each stanza and moves on at the reader's own pace, with optional tanpura, harmonium, bansuri or temple bells; a realistic mala of rudraksha, tulsi, crystal or sandalwood beads counts jaap with a soft click, haptics and the volume keys; and an aarti mode lets you wave the diya thali and ring the bell. A sadhana record keeps days in a row, paths, reading time and Gita progress. It is completely free, with no ads, no login, and works without internet.

What is different

  • Path Mode: the stanza to read lights up and moves on at your pace (slow, regular, fast or your own), with an optional repeat count and sankalp
  • A mala that behaves like a real one: beads on a thread, turn at the sumeru, count with volume keys, dark screen for night jaap
  • Aarti mode with diya thali, bell, flowers and shankh
  • 39 devotional wallpapers from public-domain paintings by Raja Ravi Varma and others
  • Ekadashi, Pradosh, Purnima, Amavasya and festival reminders with the related katha and aarti
  • Texts checked against the Gita Press tradition and other standard versions

Privacy

  • No login and no account
  • Works offline; progress stays on the phone
  • No ads and no analytics
