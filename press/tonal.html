An AI photo editor with one-tap filters, presets and pro tools, and on-device AI Select Person. Free with no ads; optional GlowShop Pro.

About

GlowShop (formerly Tonal) is an Android photo editor that makes every photo glow. Start with one of 20+ built-in looks or Auto enhance, then fine-tune with the tools desktop editors use: light, HSL, colour grading, tone curves, healing and masks. "AI Select Person" uses on-device AI (Google ML Kit selfie segmentation) to find the person in a photo in one tap, so you can brighten them or blur the background; the photo is not uploaded. GlowShop imports the presets photographers already have: Lightroom-compatible XMP and DNG presets and .cube LUTs, one by one or as a .zip. Edits are live and reversible, history is saved with each photo, and exports are full resolution with no watermark. GlowShop is free with no ads. Optional GlowShop Pro adds unlimited preset import, sky, colour and brightness masks, batch paste and export, and Instagram borders, as a yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial or a one-time lifetime purchase.

What is different

  • AI Select Person and AI Select Background in one tap, running on the phone (Google ML Kit)
  • Imports XMP/DNG presets (incl. .zip packs) and .cube 3D LUTs with a strength slider; save any edit as your own look
  • Masks that can be added, subtracted or intersected: AI person/background, radial and linear free; sky, colour and brightness with Pro
  • Full history saved with each photo, so undo still works the next day
  • Export at full original resolution, JPEG/PNG/WebP, keep or remove location and metadata
  • With GlowShop Pro: paste an edit onto many photos and export them together, Instagram borders

Privacy

  • Photos are edited on the phone and not uploaded; the app has no internet permission
  • AI Select Person runs on the device
  • No account and no sign-up
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play
  • No ads and no analytics
