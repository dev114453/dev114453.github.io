Tonal: Photo Editor & Presets

A free pro photo editor that imports XMP/DNG presets and .cube LUTs. No ads, no subscription, offline.

About

Tonal is a free Android photo editor that opens the presets photographers already have. It imports presets in XMP and DNG format (the format Lightroom presets use), straight from a file or a .zip pack, and .cube LUTs up to 64x64x64 for film and cinematic colour. On top of that are the tools of a desktop raw developer: light, HSL, three-way colour grading, tone curves, Subject, Background, Sky, Radial, Linear, colour-range and luminance-range masks, healing, and copy-paste of edits onto many photos with batch export. Every tool is unlocked, there is no subscription, watermark, ads or account, exports are full resolution, and the app has no internet access.

What is different

  • Imports XMP/DNG presets (incl. .zip packs): exposure, tone, white balance, HSL, colour grading, split toning, curves, clarity, texture, dehaze, vignette, grain and sharpening carry over
  • .cube 3D LUT import with a strength slider; save any edit as your own look
  • Seven mask types that can be added, subtracted or intersected
  • Copy edits from one photo, paste selected groups onto many, batch export at full quality
  • History saved with each photo, so undo still works the next day
  • Export at full original resolution, JPEG/PNG/WebP, metadata keep/remove location/remove all, Instagram borders

Privacy

  • No internet access, no cloud, no account
  • Photos never leave the phone
  • No ads and no analytics
