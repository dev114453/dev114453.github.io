    How to compress a PDF to 500 KB

    1. Tap Choose PDF (or drop the file on the box). The 500 KB limit is already selected.
    2. For scanned forms and certificates, open More options and tick Grayscale: black and white pages take far less space.
    3. Tap Compress PDF. The tool tries different image qualities and resolutions and keeps the best one that lands just under 500 KB.
    4. Check the new size, then tap Download PDF. Open it once to make sure every page is readable before you upload it.

    Where a 500 KB limit comes up

    500 KB is a typical cap for resumes and supporting documents on job portals, for scanned applications on state service portals, and for attachments in some online grievance and RTI systems. Colleges often allow 500 KB for transcripts.

    How many pages fit in 500 KB?

    About 5–8 pages in colour or 10–20 pages in grayscale. A short colour brochure or a multi-page transcript fits comfortably.

    Tips if the PDF won’t go under 500 KB

    • If it doesn’t fit, check for photo-heavy pages; one large photo can use as much as five pages of text.
    • Grayscale nearly halves the size of most scans.
    • For a resume made in Word or Google Docs, export it as PDF again: it is usually far below 500 KB already, with selectable text.

    Questions

    My resume PDF got bigger. Why?

    Resumes exported from a word processor are already compact and contain real text. Turning pages into images makes them larger, so the tool keeps the original. Upload it as it is.

    Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

    No. The PDF is opened and compressed by your browser on your own device. The page loads its code from this site and makes no other network requests, has no analytics and sets no cookies. You can even switch on airplane mode after the page has loaded.

    Will the text in my PDF stay selectable?

    Not in the web version. To hit a size limit reliably, each page is redrawn as an image, so text becomes part of the picture. It still reads fine, but you cannot select or search it. The FitMyPDF Android app can shrink only the images inside a PDF and leave the text as text.
