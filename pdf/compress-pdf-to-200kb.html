    How to compress a PDF to 200 KB

    1. Tap Choose PDF (or drop the file on the box). The 200 KB limit is already selected.
    2. For scanned forms and certificates, open More options and tick Grayscale: black and white pages take far less space.
    3. Tap Compress PDF. The tool tries different image qualities and resolutions and keeps the best one that lands just under 200 KB.
    4. Check the new size, then tap Download PDF. Open it once to make sure every page is readable before you upload it.

    Where a 200 KB limit comes up

    200 KB is a common document limit on Indian government and university application portals. It is also common as the upper end of a range, for example “between 100 KB and 200 KB”. If your form gives a range, open More options and set the minimum too.

    How many pages fit in 200 KB?

    Expect 2–3 pages in colour or 4–8 pages in grayscale with clear text. A one-page colour document at 200 KB looks nearly like the original.

    Tips if the PDF won’t go under 200 KB

    • Set a minimum in More options if the portal rejects files that are too small.
    • Scanned at 600 dpi? That is fine: the tool picks the resolution itself.
    • Several documents in one file? Merge them first with merge PDF, then compress the result.

    Questions

    How long does it take?

    A few seconds for a short document on a phone or laptop. Long files take longer because every page is redrawn; a progress bar shows where it is and you can cancel at any time.

    Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

    No. The PDF is opened and compressed by your browser on your own device. The page loads its code from this site and makes no other network requests, has no analytics and sets no cookies. You can even switch on airplane mode after the page has loaded.

    Will the text in my PDF stay selectable?

    Not in the web version. To hit a size limit reliably, each page is redrawn as an image, so text becomes part of the picture. It still reads fine, but you cannot select or search it. The PDF Crush Android app can shrink only the images inside a PDF and leave the text as text.
